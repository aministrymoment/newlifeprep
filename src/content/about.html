Our Founding Story

Born From a Father's Burden

New Life Preparatory Academy was born not from a business plan, but from a father's pain and a pastor's purpose.

Dr. Alex L. Jenkins Sr. was born in Ft. Pierce, Florida and raised in Cocoa, Florida. When his sons were pushed out of the public school system, not by their own inability, but by adults who chose to be roadblocks instead of open doors, a father's burden became a founder's calling.

His sons became the first graduates of what would become New Life Preparatory Academy. They were not statistics. They were pioneers. And they opened doors for hundreds of young men and women to follow.

For 25 years, NLPA operated quietly, word of mouth, no advertising, no fanfare, serving every student who walked through the door. In 2026, that faithful season gave way to a new chapter.

"I was given a second chance in life. I want to make sure every other child is given that same opportunity."

DR. ALEX L. JENKINS SR., FOUNDER

Mission · Vision · Values

Our Foundation

MISSION STATEMENT

New Life Preparatory Academy exists to provide Christ centered secondary education and diploma completion pathways to students ages 16 and older, equipping them academically, spiritually, and vocationally to fulfill their God given purpose.

VISION STATEMENT

To be the leading Christ centered alternative education institution in South Florida, where every student, regardless of their past, finds an open door, a second chance, and a future rooted in faith and excellence.
