OUR STORY 2000

"This program was built because my sons were pushed out of public school by adults who were supposed to have their best interest at heart. They are the first graduates, and they opened doors for hundreds to follow."

New Life Preparatory Academy was born not from a business plan, but from a father's pain and a pastor's purpose. Dr. Alex L. Jenkins Sr. was born in Ft. Pierce and raised in Cocoa, Florida. When his sons were pushed out of the public school system, he opened a door himself.

For 25 years, NLPA quietly served hundreds of students through word of mouth alone. In 2026, that chapter gave way to a new one: a formal board, a curriculum built on the Florida State Standards with a Christian foundation, and a commitment to accreditation.

The door is still open. It always will be.

Dr. Alex L. Jenkins Sr.

Chief Apostle · Chairman of the Board & Executive Director

Financial Assistance

Florida Scholarship Programs

NLPA participates in Florida's leading private school scholarship programs. Scholarships can cover most of academic tuition, and families pay only the remaining balance. Contact us to learn which programs you may qualify for.

Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC)

For income qualifying families seeking a private school alternative. Can cover most of tuition.

Family Empowerment Scholarship, EO (FES EO)

For income qualifying families seeking educational options outside the public school system.

Family Empowerment Scholarship, UA (FES UA)

For students with unique abilities or special learning needs requiring individualized support.

Hope Scholarship

For students who have experienced bullying, violence, or harassment in a Florida public school.

Opportunity Scholarship

For students zoned for low performing Florida public schools seeking a better educational option.

NLPA Internal Scholarships

NLPA also offers internal scholarship consideration and payment plan options. Contact the Executive Director directly and confidentially.
