Step by Step

How to Make Your Payment

1

Choose Your Payment Method

Choose what you are paying for in the payment form above and enter your name, email, and Student ID or program.

2

Select the Correct Payment

Pick the payment that matches your program track and payment plan. Refer to the fee schedule above if you are unsure.

3

Enter Your Details

Enrolled students enter their name and Student ID. New applicants enter their name and program. Donations require name only.

4

Complete Checkout

Stripe emails your payment receipt right away. If you have any question about your payment, email newlifeprep@gmail.com or call (954) 851-6608.

5

Receive Your Receipt

NLPA records your payment to your account and sends your official receipt. Keep all payment receipts for your records, they may be needed for scholarship applications and financial records.
