Financial Assistance

Florida Scholarship Programs

New Life Preparatory Academy participates in Florida's leading private school scholarship programs. Scholarships can cover most of academic tuition, and families pay only the remaining balance and fees. Award amounts are set by the State of Florida each year and vary by program and county.

NLPA will assist any family through the scholarship application process at no additional charge. Contact us and we will walk you through every step.
🎓

Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC)

One of Florida's largest private school scholarship programs. Provides funding for qualifying families to attend private schools of their choice. NLPA is a participating school.

WHO QUALIFIES: Income qualifying families

AMOUNT

Up to $8,000+/yr

🎓

Family Empowerment Scholarship, Educational Options (FES EO)

Provides scholarships for students from lower income families to attend a participating private school. Covers a significant portion or all of tuition.

WHO QUALIFIES: Income qualifying families

AMOUNT

Up to $8,000+/yr

🎓

Family Empowerment Scholarship, Unique Abilities (FES UA)

Formerly the Gardiner and McKay scholarships. For students with unique abilities or special learning needs who require individualized support and instruction.

WHO QUALIFIES: Students with unique learning needs

AMOUNT

Up to $10,000+/yr

🌟

Hope Scholarship

For students who have experienced bullying, violence, or harassment in a Florida public school. Provides funding to transfer to a participating private school.

WHO QUALIFIES: Students affected by bullying or violence

AMOUNT

Varies by grade

🚀

Opportunity Scholarship

For students zoned for low performing Florida public schools. Provides the opportunity to attend a participating private school like NLPA instead.

WHO QUALIFIES: Students zoned for D or F schools

AMOUNT

Varies by grade

Internal Assistance

NLPA Internal Scholarships

NLPA's mission is to serve students, finances should never be a permanent barrier to education. In addition to Florida scholarship programs, NLPA offers internal assistance options for qualifying students.

These options are handled directly, personally, and confidentially by the Executive Director. They are not publicly advertised, but they are real, and they are available.

Contact the Executive Director
Payment Plans

Split payments across enrollment period. Minimum 50% at enrollment, balance before diploma issuance.

Extended Payment Plans

Monthly installment agreements available by written arrangement with the Executive Director.

Internal Scholarship Consideration

Partial scholarship awards based on financial need, academic commitment, and character. Handled confidentially.

Community Service Hours

Students with genuine financial hardship may satisfy a portion of tuition through approved community service hours.
