It Is Never Too Late to Graduate

The NLPA Adult Diploma Completion Program is designed for individuals ages 16 and older who began their secondary education but were unable to complete it. You worked hard. You earned credits. Life happened. NLPA is here to help you finish what you started.

Ages 16 to 17 welcome with written Parental or Legal Guardian Consent. Students ages 16 to 17 must also provide proof of formal withdrawal from their previous school.

How the Program Works

1

Inquiry & Enrollment Consultation

Contact NLPA to schedule a consultation. We review the program, requirements, and costs together.

2

Application & Student ID

Complete the NLPA Enrollment Application and pay the $50 application fee. You receive your NLPA Student ID.

3

Transcript Evaluation

Submit your transcript from previous school(s). NLPA evaluates your credits and prepares a personalized Course Assignment Sheet.

4

Coursework Completion

Complete your assigned courses through NLPA's hybrid program, self paced online plus biweekly advisor support.

5

Biblical Foundation

All students complete Old Testament Survey and New Testament Survey, a core part of every NLPA diploma.

6

Diploma Issuance

Once all requirements are met and tuition is paid in full, your official NLPA diploma and transcript are issued within 7 to 10 business days.

Also available: Adult Diploma Assessment Program, $750.00. For adults ages 16 and older who already have at least 16 earned credits on an official transcript. Complete five proctored assessments, four subject tests and a 500 word essay, aligned to the Florida State Standards. Score 70 percent or better on each, and retake any assessment until you pass. Proctoring is included in the program price. Application fee $50.00 (waivable). Your official diploma and transcript are issued within 7 to 10 business days after all requirements are met.
