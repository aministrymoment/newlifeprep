How to Enroll

Admissions Process

NLPA welcomes students who are committed to completing their education in a Christ centered environment. Here is exactly how to get started.

1

Contact NLPA

Call (954) 851-6608 or email newlifeprep@gmail.com to schedule your enrollment consultation. We respond within 2 business days.

2

Enrollment Consultation

Meet with the Executive Director in person, by phone, or video. We review your background, goals, and the right program for you.

3

Submit Application

Complete the NLPA Enrollment Application and pay the $50 nonrefundable application fee. You will receive your NLPA Student ID.

4

Submit Transcripts

Provide transcripts from all previously attended accredited schools. Students without transcripts may still apply, see below.

5

Course Assignment

NLPA evaluates your transcripts within 5 business days and prepares your personalized Course Assignment Sheet.

6

Pay Tuition & Begin

Pay your tuition (or first installment). Receive your curriculum access, NLPA login, and official welcome packet. Begin your courses.
