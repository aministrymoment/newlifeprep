The Vision

Athletics at NLPA

New Life Preparatory Academy was not built on academics alone. From the very beginning, athletics was part of the vision, because Dr. Alex L. Jenkins Sr. understood that a young person's confidence, discipline, teamwork, and sense of belonging can be forged on a court or a field just as powerfully as in a classroom.

When Dr. Jenkins' sons were pushed out of the public school system, denied not just an academic pathway but an athletic one, the dream of NLPA included restoring both. The sons who became NLPA's first graduates carried with them not just diplomas, but the understanding that their potential had never been the problem.

Every NLPA student athlete who puts on a Camels uniform carries that history.

Academic enrollment at NLPA is required for all student athletes. The NLPA Camel competes in the classroom first. Athletics is a privilege earned through academic commitment.

Why the Camel?

The camel carries heavy loads across long, hard terrain without giving up. It is built for the journey that others abandon. It does not quit when the conditions get difficult, it was made for exactly those conditions.

That is the NLPA student athlete.

Biblical Significance

In Scripture, the camel represents provision, blessing, and the faithfulness of God to those who trust Him on long journeys. The NLPA Camels carry that legacy into every competition.
